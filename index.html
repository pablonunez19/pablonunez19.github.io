About Me

I'm Pablo Núñez Martínez, a master student in Aeronautics & Astronautics at Stanford, I did my bachelors degree in Mechanical Engineering at Tec de Monterrey, with an exchange experience at McGill University.

I specialise in aerodynamic design, CFD and FEA simulation, and carbon fiber composite manufacturing. As founder of ELYOS Racing Team, I led 50+ students across four competition vehicles, including Mexico's first-ever carbon fiber monocoque for Shell Eco-marathon.

Currently serving as Aerodynamics Lead on Sunstruck, Stanford Solar Car Project's vehicle for the 2027 World Solar Challenge, trying to design one of the most efficient cars in the team's history.
